## [1.0] 2026-11-24

- HDB-DOS 1.5 (DriveWire over the Becker port) as a 16K ROM for the CoCo 2 and CoCo 3
- `CONFIG` and `LOBBY` reboot into FujiNet CONFIG and the game lobby
- `TIME$`, `DATE$`, `GUID$` and `IP$` from the FujiNet
- `UCASE$`, `LCASE$`, `TRIM$`, `URLENCODE$` and `FIELD$`
- Network files: `OPEN`, `PRINT #`, `INPUT #`, `LINE INPUT #`, `EOF`, `LOF` and `CLOSE` on `N:` names, with `HEADER`, `NREAD$`, `NETERR` and JSON, XML and HTML queries
- `FPRINT` sends printer output to the FujiNet printer
- `BACKUP #`, `DSKINI #` and `COPY` with `:#n` names work on the images in the FujiNet drives
- `QRCODE` draws a QR code on the 32-column screen
- `CPUSPEED` prints or sets a CoCo 3's CPU speed
- Test disk with DEMO, WEATHER, DADJOKE, NETCAT, QRDEMO and NETTEST
